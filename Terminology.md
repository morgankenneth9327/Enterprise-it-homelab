# Technical Terminology

## Host

A physical or virtual computer providing resources or running workloads.

## Hypervisor

Software that creates and manages virtual machines.

## Provisioning

The process of creating and configuring a resource for use.

## Authentication

Verification of an identity.

## Authorization

Determination of what an authenticated identity is permitted to access.

## Baseline

The documented starting state used for comparison after changes.

## Validation

Testing performed to verify that a change produced the intended result.
